Karyn Walker

Director | Registered Architect | ANZIA | MArch. (Prof) | BAS.

Karyn is passionate about residential architecture. She believes it needs to be functional, sustainable and beautiful with a pleasant ambience and sense of space, all contributing to her clients' individual requirements and experiences.

It is important to Karyn that her clients' requirements are well-defined and understood while considering how she can add further value to their projects.

She focuses on integrating architectural form with interior spaces and the landscape, also towards accessibility within the design - considering spaces that work for people of any ability.

With a practical, hands-on approach, Karyn was involved throughout the construction, additions and alterations to her own home.

Karyn looks forward to working with you on your bespoke project.
