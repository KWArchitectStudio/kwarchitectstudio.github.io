Residential Addition & Alteration · Kapiti Coast

Living Extension

An existing home on the Kapiti Coast requires an extension to create a larger indoor living space, improved outdoor flow to accommodate a family with young children and a wheelchair user, meaning accessibility is important.

Level entry access through stacking sliding doors, exterior ramps, space around and between indoor furniture for manoeuvring, with lower storage considerations for wheelchair accessibility.

Location
Kapiti Coast, New Zealand
Project Type
Residential Addition & Alteration
Scope
Living space extension, accessible indoor-outdoor flow
